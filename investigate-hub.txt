  FINDING

    · It is NOT concentrated in a few people (top-3 share 6% vs peer 25%) — rules out "a few bad armadores"; whatever this is, it's hub-wide.

    · Cross-hub, "role-account share of assembly" discriminates (mh_contry 0.02 vs peer 0.04, rank-correlation -0.79) — a lead, not proof (n=7 hubs).
